# Über die Bestimmung der Richtung der durch elektrodynamische Vertheilung erregten galvanischen Ströme

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR C's QA pass.*

Lenz's law: the direction of an induced current is always such that its own magnetic field *opposes* the change that induced it. Read together with Faraday (1832), Lenz fixes the *sign* in $\mathcal{E} = -d\Phi/dt$. Conceptually decisive: without the negative sign, perpetual motion would result from coupling induction to a no-loss mechanical-electrical converter. Lenz's law is the early-19th-century version of energy conservation applied to electromagnetism, predating Helmholtz's *Über die Erhaltung der Kraft* (1847) by 13 years.

`#gill-silent` — sign convention is preserved by both standard and dual Maxwell equations.
