# Ist die Trägheit eines Körpers von seinem Energieinhalt abhängig?

*Skeleton bib note.* Three-page sequel to the special-relativity paper, deriving $E = mc^2$ from a thought-experiment about energy loss via photon emission in two reference frames. The mass-energy equivalence is foundational for nuclear physics ([[09_fusion_open_questions|Chapter 9]] fusion energy release) and for the rest-energy term in the dual-framework Hamiltonian $K = H^2/(2mc^2) + mc^2/2$ that appears in [[FoundationsII-Classical]].
