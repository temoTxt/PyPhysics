# Sagnac effect

*Skeleton bib note.* Sagnac's demonstration that a rotating interferometer produces a fringe shift $\Delta\phi = 8\pi A \Omega/(\lambda c)$ where $A$ is the enclosed area and $\Omega$ the angular velocity. The Sagnac effect is the basis for ring-laser and fiber-optic gyroscopes and enters GPS positioning as a correction for the Earth's rotation during signal transit. The dual framework reproduces the standard Sagnac formula at all current precision (the rotating-frame source is at non-relativistic velocity).
