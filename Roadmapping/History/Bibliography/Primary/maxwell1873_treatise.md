# A Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

Maxwell's two-volume systematic treatise, written eight years after the *Dynamical Theory*. The equations are recast using quaternion notation (later replaced by Heaviside and Gibbs's vector calculus). The Treatise is the working physicist's reference throughout the late 19th century and the source most readers of Maxwell actually consulted — the 1865 *Dynamical Theory* being relatively obscure until Heaviside's reformulation circulated. **Public-domain first edition (1873)** scanned and available on archive.org.
