# Gravity Probe A (Vessot et al.)

*Skeleton bib note.* Vessot's 1976 sub-orbital hydrogen-maser experiment confirming GR gravitational time dilation to ~70 ppm. The rocket-borne maser's clock rate, compared to a ground-based identical maser, varies with altitude in agreement with the Schwarzschild prediction. Validates GR's clock predictions at the precision relevant to GPS engineering.
