# On the Production of Currents and Sparks of Electricity from Magnetism

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR C's QA pass.*

Henry's independent discovery of electromagnetic induction at the Albany Academy, made in summer 1831 — *before* Faraday's published account but unpublished until 1832 due to teaching obligations. The priority dispute is mostly resolved in Faraday's favor on the basis of publication date; Henry, who had no axe to grind, ungrudgingly acknowledged Faraday's priority. Henry's distinctive contribution: identification of *self-inductance* (the spark seen when breaking the circuit of a long-wound coil), now memorialized in the SI unit of inductance.
