# Cathode Rays

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

J. J. Thomson's announcement of the electron. By deflecting cathode rays in crossed electric and magnetic fields and measuring the resulting trajectory, he extracts $e/m$ — finding a value roughly 1,800 times larger than that for the lightest known ion (hydrogen). Concludes the carrier is a subatomic *corpuscle*, common to all matter, with a mass much smaller than the atom. Confirms Lorentz's electron-theory hypothesis (1892) experimentally. Conceptually closes Chapter 2 and opens Chapter 3 — the existence of the electron is the prerequisite for the entire quantum-theory programme. `#gill-silent` — the discovery itself is classical kinematics in lab frame.
