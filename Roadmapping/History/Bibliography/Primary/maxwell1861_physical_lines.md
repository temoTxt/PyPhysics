# On Physical Lines of Force

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

Three-part serial paper in which Maxwell constructs an elaborate mechanical-aether model (vortices and idle wheels) and, working through its internal consistency, discovers the **displacement current** term $\partial_t \mathbf{D}$ that must be added to Ampère's law to make the equations consistent with charge conservation. Once the displacement current is in place, the equations support transverse wave propagation with phase velocity $1/\sqrt{\mu_0 \varepsilon_0}$ — numerically very close to the speed of light. Maxwell concludes (Part III, 1862): "we can scarcely avoid the inference that light consists in the transverse undulations of the same medium which is the cause of electric and magnetic phenomena." The 1865 paper drops the mechanical scaffolding and presents the equations standalone.
