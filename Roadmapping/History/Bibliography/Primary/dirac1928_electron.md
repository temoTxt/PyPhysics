# The Quantum Theory of the Electron

*Skeleton bib note.* Dirac's relativistic equation $(i\gamma^\mu \partial_\mu - mc/\hbar)\psi = 0$ for spin-1/2 particles. First-order in time derivatives (unlike Klein–Gordon), giving a positive-definite probability density $\psi^\dagger \psi$. Predicts (i) electron spin-1/2 emerges naturally from the 4-spinor structure, no longer requiring the *ad hoc* Uhlenbeck–Goudsmit postulate; (ii) g-factor $g = 2$ for the electron, matching experiment; (iii) fine structure of hydrogen with all relativistic + spin–orbit corrections built in; (iv) negative-energy solutions which Dirac reinterprets (1930–31) as the *positron* hole, confirmed by [[anderson1932_positron|Anderson 1932]]. **Verification anchor** for this chapter: [[Dual_Relativistic_Quantum_Mechanics_I]] provides the dual Dirac equation $K_D = (c\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot \mathbf{p} + \beta m c^2)^2/(2mc^2) + mc^2/2$ — a positive-definite squared form — and **Finding 2** (Eq. (III.22)) — flagged in [[FINDINGS_for_author_review]] — concerns the g-factor formula for the dual Dirac model.
