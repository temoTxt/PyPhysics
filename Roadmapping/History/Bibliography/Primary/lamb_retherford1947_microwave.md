# Fine Structure of the Hydrogen Atom by a Microwave Method

*Skeleton bib note.* Lamb and Retherford's measurement (using wartime radar microwave technology) of a ~1000 MHz splitting between the $2S_{1/2}$ and $2P_{1/2}$ states of hydrogen — states that Dirac's relativistic theory predicts to be exactly degenerate. The "Lamb shift" — small but unambiguous. Triggers the QED renormalization revolution: [[bethe1947_lamb_shift_calc|Bethe's non-relativistic calculation]] within weeks, then Schwinger, Tomonaga, Feynman, Dyson independently. The Lamb shift is the first experimental confirmation of QED radiative corrections; the dual framework's QED reframing via [[FOUNDATIONS_FOR_QED_I_MATHEMATICAL|Foundations I]] gives equivalent renormalised predictions.
