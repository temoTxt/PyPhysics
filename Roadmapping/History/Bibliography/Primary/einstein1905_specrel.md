# Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Körper

*Skeleton bib note.* Special relativity. Postulates the constancy of `c` and the equivalence of inertial frames; derives the Lorentz transformations (which had been written by [[lorentz1892_electron_theory|Lorentz]] as a calculational device) as *kinematic* consequences. Length contraction, time dilation, relativistic velocity addition all follow. **The dual-theory contrast** ([[Two_Mathematically_Equivalent_Versions_of_Maxwells_Equations]]): standard SR's Lorentz boosts are replaced by velocity-duality boosts $\mathbf{w}/c = \mathbf{u}/b$, and length contraction is *not* an independent kinematic postulate — it falls out of the velocity-duality structure. Predictions coincide at all current measurement precisions; the conceptual difference is in interpretation. `#inferred` because the dual framework reproduces every standard-SR prediction (Doppler, time dilation, mass-energy) via the $b = \sqrt{c^2+u^2}$ rescaling.
