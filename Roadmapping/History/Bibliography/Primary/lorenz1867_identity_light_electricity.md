# On the Identity of the Vibrations of Light with Electrical Currents

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

Ludvig Lorenz (Danish — not to be confused with the Dutch Hendrik Lorentz of 1892+) independently derives that EM disturbances propagate at the speed of light, two years after Maxwell. Works in the framework of retarded action-at-a-distance rather than a continuous field, introducing what we now call the **Lorenz gauge** ($\partial_\mu A^\mu = 0$) — the most natural gauge condition for retarded potentials. Often subsumed under Maxwell's name in textbooks; Whittaker (1910) Ch IX recovers the priority correctly.
