# Quantum cryptography based on Bell's theorem (E91)

*Skeleton bib note.* Ekert's entanglement-based alternative to BB84: Alice and Bob share entangled pairs; the security argument uses Bell's inequality rather than the no-cloning theorem. Operationally similar to BB84 but conceptually rooted in entanglement.
