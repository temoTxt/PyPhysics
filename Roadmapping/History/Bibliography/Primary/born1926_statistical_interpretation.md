# Zur Quantenmechanik der Stoßvorgänge

*Skeleton bib note.* Born's *statistical interpretation* of the wave function: $|\psi(\mathbf{x})|^2$ is the probability density for finding the particle at position $\mathbf{x}$. Resolves the conceptual difficulty Schrödinger himself faced of what $\psi$ *means* physically — Schrödinger had hoped it would describe a real charge density wave. The Born rule becomes the operational backbone of QM measurement; the interpretive debates (Bohr/Heisenberg Copenhagen vs. Einstein/EPR/Bell, decades later) are about *why* the rule holds, not whether it does.
