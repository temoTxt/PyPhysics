# Experimental Test of Parity Conservation in Beta Decay

*Skeleton bib note.* Wu and collaborators at the NBS Low Temperature Laboratory align Co-60 nuclei at $\sim 10$ mK and measure beta-emission directional asymmetry relative to the nuclear spin. The asymmetry is *maximal* — parity is *not* conserved in weak interactions. The result, [[lee_yang1956_parity_question|Lee and Yang's proposed test]] turned positive within months, ends a 30-year-old assumption about the symmetries of physical law. Confirmed independently by Garwin–Lederman–Weinrich at Columbia using muon decay within weeks. Establishes the V–A structure of the weak interaction.
