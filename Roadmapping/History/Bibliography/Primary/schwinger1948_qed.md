# On Quantum Electrodynamics and the Magnetic Moment of the Electron

*Skeleton bib note.* Schwinger's first-order QED calculation of the electron anomalous magnetic moment, $a_e = (g-2)/2 = \alpha/(2\pi) \approx 0.001162$. Agrees with the contemporary Foley–Kusch (1947, 1948) measurements. Schwinger's covariant operator-formalism approach, alongside [[tomonaga1946_qed|Tomonaga's]] and [[feynman1949_diagrams|Feynman's]] independent formulations, becomes the basis for modern QED. [[dyson1949_equivalence|Dyson 1949]] proves the three formulations equivalent. The dual-framework's QED rederivation in [[FOUNDATIONS_FOR_QED_I_MATHEMATICAL]] reproduces Schwinger's $\alpha/(2\pi)$ at the same order.
