# Question of Parity Conservation in Weak Interactions

*Skeleton bib note.* Lee and Yang's review of the $\theta$–$\tau$ puzzle: two particles with identical mass and lifetime decay into final states of opposite parity. Concludes that *no experiment had ever tested* parity conservation in weak interactions — assumed by everyone, never measured. Proposes specific tests, especially aligning Co-60 nuclei and looking for an asymmetry in beta-emission direction relative to spin. [[wu1957_parity|Wu's experiment]] within a year confirms maximal parity violation. Nobel prize 1957.
