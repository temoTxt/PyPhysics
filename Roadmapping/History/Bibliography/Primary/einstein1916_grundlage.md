# Die Grundlage der allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie

*Skeleton bib note.* The systematic exposition of general relativity, written after the November 1915 field equations and their successful application to Mercury's perihelion advance. Einstein's field equations $G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}/c^4$ couple matter-energy content to spacetime curvature. Foundational for [[07_PNT_GPS_SLR_QKD|Chapter 7's]] GR clock-correction derivation that GPS depends on. `#gill-silent` for the dual framework: Gill has not published an extension of the dual-theory formalism to GR; the GR predictions for Mercury, GPS clocks, and Shapiro delay are taken intact in the dual framework, but with no proper-time-framework re-derivation available.
