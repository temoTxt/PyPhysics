# Versuch einer Theorie der β-Strahlen. I

*Skeleton bib note.* Fermi's theory of beta decay: $n \to p + e^- + \bar{\nu}_e$, mediated by a four-fermion contact interaction with coupling constant $G_F$. The first quantitative theory of the *weak interaction*. Incorporates Pauli's 1930 neutrino hypothesis (introduced to save energy conservation in beta decay) as a genuine particle that is now part of the interaction Lagrangian. Will be replaced by the GWS electroweak gauge theory in 1967–68 but remains the *low-energy* limit of weak processes; the Fermi coupling $G_F$ measures the W-boson exchange amplitude in the low-momentum limit. Foundational also for stellar fusion ([[09_fusion_open_questions|Chapter 9]]): the slow first step of the pp-chain ($p + p \to {}^2\!H + e^+ + \nu_e$) is governed by the weak interaction.
