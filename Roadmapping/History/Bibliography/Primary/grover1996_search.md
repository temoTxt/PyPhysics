# Grover's search algorithm

*Skeleton bib note.* Grover's quantum algorithm for unstructured search: $O(\sqrt{N})$ queries to find a marked item among $N$, vs. $O(N)$ classically. Quadratic speedup — modest by Shor's standards, but applies to a broad class of NP-search problems. Optimality proven by Bennett–Bernstein–Brassard–Vazirani (1997).
