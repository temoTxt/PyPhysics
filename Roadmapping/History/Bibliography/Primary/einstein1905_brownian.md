# Brownian-motion paper

*Skeleton bib note.* Einstein's prediction that suspended particles undergo a random walk with mean-square displacement $\langle x^2 \rangle = 2Dt$, where the diffusion constant $D = k_B T / (6\pi \eta r)$. Experimental verification by Perrin (1908) becomes the decisive evidence for the atomic theory of matter against the lingering energeticist tradition (Mach, Ostwald). Indirect bearing on quantum-theory development: legitimises statistical reasoning about microscopic constituents.
