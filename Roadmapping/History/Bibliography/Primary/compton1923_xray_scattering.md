# A Quantum Theory of the Scattering of X-rays by Light Elements

*Skeleton bib note.* Compton's demonstration that X-ray scattering off (effectively) free electrons shifts wavelength by $\Delta\lambda = (h/m_e c)(1 - \cos\theta)$ — the *Compton wavelength* of the electron multiplied by the angular factor. Direct experimental evidence that photons carry momentum $p = h/\lambda$, vindicating Einstein's 1916 conjecture. Inertial free-electron Compton scattering is reproduced identically in the dual framework — for a free electron with $u/c \sim 0.01$ at typical X-ray scattering kinematics, the $b/c$ correction is below $10^{-4}$, beneath Compton's apparatus precision. `#inferred`: dual framework gives the same shift formula; the conceptual difference (whether momentum conservation is between two 4-momenta in standard SR or between two proper-time-parameterised states in the dual framework) is interpretive.
