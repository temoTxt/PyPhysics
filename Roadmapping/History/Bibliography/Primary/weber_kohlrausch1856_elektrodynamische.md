# Elektrodynamische Maassbestimmungen — ratio of electromagnetic to electrostatic units

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

Weber and Kohlrausch's experimental determination of the ratio of electromagnetic to electrostatic units of charge. The ratio comes out very near $3 \times 10^{10}$ cm/s — the speed of light — a coincidence that Kirchhoff exploits the following year (1857) for transmission-line analysis and that Maxwell turns into the unification of electromagnetism with optics in 1865. The measurement itself is an heroic exercise in absolute-units metrology (in Gauss's 1832 tradition), using a current balance and a Leyden-jar capacitance comparison.
