# Über das Gravitationsfeld eines Massenpunktes — Schwarzschild metric

*Skeleton bib note.* Schwarzschild's exact solution of Einstein's vacuum field equations for a spherically symmetric mass — written within weeks of Einstein's GR paper, while Schwarzschild was on the Russian front. Gives $ds^2 = (1 - 2GM/c^2 r)c^2 dt^2 - (1 - 2GM/c^2 r)^{-1} dr^2 - r^2 d\Omega^2$. Foundational for Mercury perihelion, GPS gravitational time dilation, black-hole physics, Shapiro delay.
