# Mémoire sur les effets des courants électriques

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR C's QA pass.*

Ampère's response to Ørsted: parallel currents in the same direction attract; antiparallel repel. Three weeks of frantic experimentation in Paris produced the force law $F \propto I_1 I_2 / r$ between current elements, the conception of magnetism as a manifestation of moving charges (the "Ampèrian current" model), and the term *electrodynamics*. Compiled and re-derived in the more careful 1826 *Théorie mathématique*, but the September–October 1820 communications are the discovery itself.

`#gill-silent` — magnetostatic force law; observer-frame currents only, no proper-time content. The same algebra holds in standard SR and dual SR.
