# Über die Erhaltung der Kraft

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

Helmholtz's seventy-page monograph stating conservation of energy as a universal principle — generalising the pieces visible in mechanics (Mayer 1842, Joule 1843), thermodynamics, chemistry, and electromagnetism (Lenz 1834 was the EM precursor). Reformulates electromagnetism in Lagrangian terms and provides Maxwell's mechanical-aether models (1861, 1865) with a conceptual scaffold. Included in this chapter primarily as the conceptual prerequisite for the Hamiltonian formulation that the dual framework will reframe via $K = H^2/(2mc^2) + mc^2/2$ in [[FoundationsII-Classical]].
