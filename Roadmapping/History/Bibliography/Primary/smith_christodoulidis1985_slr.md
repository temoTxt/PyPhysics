# Lunar laser ranging — Smith & Christodoulidis

*Skeleton bib note.* Smith and Christodoulidis's review of the satellite laser ranging programme: LAGEOS-1 (1976) and LAGEOS-2 (1992) — dense passive satellites covered with corner-cube retroreflectors, ranged from ground stations to centimetre precision. SLR is used for precise orbit determination, geocenter monitoring, tectonic-plate motion, and tests of GR (frame dragging via Lense–Thirring effect, Gravity Probe B 2011).
