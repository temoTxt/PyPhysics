# Über die Quantenmechanik der Elektronen in Kristallgittern

*Skeleton bib note.* Bloch's doctoral thesis: electrons in a periodic potential have wavefunctions of the form $\psi_\mathbf{k}(\mathbf{r}) = e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{r}} u_\mathbf{k}(\mathbf{r})$ with $u_\mathbf{k}$ sharing the lattice periodicity (Bloch's theorem). Solves the long-standing puzzle of why metals conduct at all (mean free paths of cm, not Å) by showing that scattering off a *perfect* periodic potential is zero. Foundation of band theory; followed up by Wilson (1931) classifying metals/semiconductors/insulators by band filling, and by Sommerfeld–Bethe (1933) for the systematic treatment. Direct line to the transistor (1947) and modern semiconductor physics.
