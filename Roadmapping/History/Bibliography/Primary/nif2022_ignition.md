# NIF ignition (Abu-Shawareb et al. 2024)

*Skeleton bib note.* The peer-reviewed report on the December 2022 NIF experiment that crossed the *ignition* threshold — fusion energy produced ($\sim 3.15$ MJ) exceeded the laser energy delivered to the target ($\sim 2.05$ MJ). The first laboratory demonstration of fusion *gain* > 1, six decades after the [[lawson1957_criterion|Lawson criterion]] was written down. **Not** practical power generation: the *plug-in* energy required to fire the lasers was ~300 MJ. But the underlying physics — inertial confinement of a deuterium-tritium capsule by indirectly-driven X-rays from a gold hohlraum — is now demonstrated.
