# Possible New Effects in Superconductive Tunnelling

*Skeleton bib note.* Josephson's prediction (made as a graduate student at Cambridge under Brian Pippard) of two effects in a tunnel junction between two superconductors:
1. **DC Josephson effect:** a supercurrent flows through the junction with zero voltage, with $I = I_c \sin\Delta\phi$ where $\Delta\phi$ is the phase difference between the two superconductors' order parameters.
2. **AC Josephson effect:** if a DC voltage $V$ is applied, the supercurrent oscillates at frequency $\omega = 2eV/\hbar$.

Experimental confirmation by Anderson and Rowell (1963), Shapiro (1963). Nobel 1973. The Josephson effect is the operational substrate of modern superconducting qubits — the *transmon* and *flux* qubits in current quantum-computing hardware all derive from Josephson junctions. Connection to [[08_quantum_computing_open_questions|Chapter 8]] is the speculative-but-concrete bridge to circuit QED.
