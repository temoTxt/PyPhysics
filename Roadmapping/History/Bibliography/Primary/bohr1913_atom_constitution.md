# On the Constitution of Atoms and Molecules

*Skeleton bib note.* Bohr's three-part series proposing quantised atomic states. Postulate 1: electrons occupy discrete circular orbits with angular momentum $L = n\hbar$. Postulate 2: transitions emit/absorb photons with $h\nu = E_m - E_n$. Reproduces the hydrogen Rydberg formula exactly; predicts the value of the Rydberg constant in terms of $m_e, e, h$. **Dual-framework re-derivation** `#inferred` from [[FoundationsII-Classical]]: replace the standard non-relativistic Hamiltonian $H$ with the positive-definite dual Hamiltonian $K = H^2/(2mc^2) + mc^2/2$; the energy levels rederived this way agree with standard Bohr at non-relativistic precision (the Bohr-orbit velocity $v/c \sim Z\alpha$ is at most a few percent for hydrogen, giving a $b/c$ correction of order $10^{-4}$ — below Rydberg-formula precision at the 1913 measurement level).
