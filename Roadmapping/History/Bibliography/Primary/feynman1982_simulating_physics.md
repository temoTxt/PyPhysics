# Simulating Physics with Computers

*Skeleton bib note.* Feynman's MIT keynote (May 1981, published 1982). Argues that simulating quantum systems on classical computers requires exponential resources, but a *quantum* computer — a system whose dynamics are governed by quantum mechanics — could simulate other quantum systems efficiently. The foundational vision-statement for quantum computing as a distinct computational paradigm.
