# Über quantentheoretische Umdeutung kinematischer und mechanischer Beziehungen

*Skeleton bib note.* Heisenberg's "Umdeutung" — *reinterpretation* — paper, written during his isolation on Helgoland in June 1925 to escape hay fever. Drops the orbital picture of Bohr–Sommerfeld; replaces position and momentum with arrays of transition amplitudes that turn out to compose non-commutatively. The genesis of matrix mechanics; Born and Jordan formalise the matrix structure in two follow-on papers (Born–Jordan 1925, the *Dreimännerarbeit* with Heisenberg in early 1926).
