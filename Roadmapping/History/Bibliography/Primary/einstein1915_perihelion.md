# Erklärung der Perihelbewegung des Merkur aus der allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie

*Skeleton bib note.* Einstein's November-1915 derivation of Mercury's anomalous 43″/century perihelion advance from his then-just-completed GR field equations. The match between the GR-predicted $\Delta\phi = 6\pi GM/(c^2 a(1-e^2)) \approx 43''/$century and [[verrier1859_mercury|Le Verrier's 1859 residual]] is GR's first observational confirmation. Will be re-derived step-by-step at grad-student level in [[07_PNT_GPS_SLR_QKD|Chapter 7 §B]] as the pedagogical bridge between Mercury's orbit and GPS satellite clock corrections.
