# Note sur le magnétisme de la pile de Volta

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR C's QA pass.*

Two-page communication in which Biot & Savart announce, weeks after Ørsted, that the magnetic field of a long straight current-carrying wire scales as $1/r$ (the distance from the wire). The line-integral form $d\mathbf{B} \propto I d\boldsymbol{\ell} \times \hat{\mathbf{r}}/r^2$ — the Biot–Savart law as we now write it — was given in a separate 1821 note after further work with Laplace. Companion to Ampère's force-law: Biot–Savart describes the *field* a current produces; Ampère's law describes the *force* a current feels.
