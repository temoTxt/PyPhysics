# Experimenta circa effectum conflictus electrici in acum magneticam

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR C's QA pass.*

The four-page pamphlet (in Latin) announcing that a current-carrying wire deflects a nearby compass needle perpendicular to the wire. Distributed to learned societies across Europe in July 1820. The first experimental link between electricity and magnetism — they had been treated as separate phenomena since antiquity. Triggers the avalanche: Ampère reads it in September, publishes his force law within weeks; Biot & Savart announce theirs in October.

`#gill-silent` — pure magnetostatics from a steady current; standard SR + dual SR agree trivially (`u = 0` for the wire's bulk frame).
