# The Internal Constitution of the Stars (Eddington 1920)

*Skeleton bib note.* Eddington's presidential address to the British Association: stars are powered by nuclear fusion of hydrogen into helium, releasing the [[aston1922_mass_defect|mass defect]] as energy via $E = mc^2$. The conjecture is bold — the underlying nuclear physics is largely speculative in 1920 — and the quantitative mechanism waits for [[bethe1939_ppchain|Bethe's pp-chain and CNO cycle (1939)]]. Eddington famously says: "What is possible in the Cavendish Laboratory may not be too difficult in the Sun."
