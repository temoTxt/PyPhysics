# Lettre de M. Le Verrier à M. Faye sur la théorie de Mercure

*Skeleton bib note.* Le Verrier's announcement of the anomalous 43″/century advance of Mercury's perihelion that Newtonian gravity + planetary perturbations cannot account for. Le Verrier himself speculates about an unseen planet (provisionally named *Vulcan*) inside Mercury's orbit; no such planet is found. The residual sits as a quantitative astronomical puzzle for 56 years until [[einstein1915_perihelion|Einstein's November 1915 paper]] derives exactly the same residual from GR. Era-classified as "1900-1925" in this bibliography because its relevance to the campaign is via Einstein 1915 — though the paper itself is 1859.
