# The Radiation Theories of Tomonaga, Schwinger, and Feynman

*Skeleton bib note.* Dyson's proof that the three independent QED formulations are mathematically equivalent. Introduces the *Dyson series* — the time-ordered perturbative expansion of the S-matrix — and shows that Feynman's diagrammatic rules are the natural calculational tool for evaluating it. The follow-on *Divergence of Perturbation Theory in Quantum Electrodynamics* ([[dyson1952_divergence]] in 1952) argues, on resummation grounds, that the QED perturbation series is *asymptotic, not convergent* — the **Dyson divergence conjecture** that Gill's [[FOUNDATIONS_FOR_QED_I_MATHEMATICAL|Foundations I]] sidesteps via the time-ordered Feynman Operator Calculus.
