# The Ether and the Earth's Atmosphere

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

FitzGerald's one-paragraph proposal in *Science* responding to Michelson–Morley: matter contracts along the direction of motion through the aether by a factor that just cancels the expected fringe shift. The proposal is *ad hoc* — no mechanism is offered — but the algebraic form is correct ($\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}$). Independently rediscovered by Lorentz (1892, 1895) who gives it a more thoroughly worked-out electron-theory motivation. Together "FitzGerald–Lorentz contraction" becomes the pre-Einstein resolution of Michelson–Morley. **Replaced** in Einstein's 1905 framework by length contraction-as-coordinate-effect, and in Gill's dual framework by the proper-time / collaborative-speed reframing that doesn't require a separate kinematic length contraction at all.
