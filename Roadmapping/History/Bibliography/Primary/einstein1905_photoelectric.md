# Über einen die Erzeugung und Verwandlung des Lichtes betreffenden heuristischen Gesichtspunkt

*Skeleton bib note.* The "light quantum" paper from Einstein's *annus mirabilis*. Argues from a statistical-mechanics analogy with the Wien-regime blackbody spectrum that light *itself* behaves like a collection of independent quanta $h\nu$, not just absorbed/emitted in quanta. The photoelectric effect follows immediately: maximum kinetic energy of ejected electrons is $E_k = h\nu - W$, where $W$ is the work function. Verified experimentally by Millikan ([[millikan1916_photoelectric_verification]]) over the next decade. `#inferred` for the dual framework: Compton-like (free-electron) processes reproduce the standard formula at any current precision; bound-state photoabsorption inherits whichever QM treatment one uses.
