# Recherches sur la théorie des quanta

*Skeleton bib note.* De Broglie's doctoral thesis: matter has a wavelength $\lambda = h/p$ symmetric with the photon's $p = h/\lambda$. Predicts electron diffraction, confirmed by Davisson–Germer (1927). Conceptually the bridge between particles (Bohr orbits with $L = n\hbar$) and waves (the next-generation Schrödinger equation). The dual framework's `#inferred` reframing: the matter-wave dispersion relation $\omega(\mathbf{k})$ for the Klein-Gordon-like square-root operator in [[Analytic_Representation_of_The_Square-Root_Operator]] reduces to the de Broglie form $\lambda = h/p$ in the non-relativistic limit, by construction.
