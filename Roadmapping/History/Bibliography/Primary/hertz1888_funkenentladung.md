# Über die Ausbreitung der electrischen Kraft

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

Hertz's experimental demonstration that electromagnetic waves *exist* — generated by a spark gap on a dipole antenna, detected meters away by a resonant secondary loop with its own gap. Measures wavelength (by standing-wave interference between direct and reflected waves) and timing; deduces propagation speed consistent with $c$. The first experimental confirmation of Maxwell's 1865 prediction. Hertz also demonstrates that EM waves can be reflected, refracted, polarised — they behave optically. Sweeps away the last serious objections to Maxwell's theory and decisively shifts the British field-tradition over the Continental action-at-a-distance tradition. `#verified` because the underlying Maxwell wave equation is the load-bearing anchor for the dual theory ([[Two_Mathematically_Equivalent_Versions_of_Maxwells_Equations]]); Hertz's measured wave behaviour is reproduced identically by both standard Maxwell and the dual Maxwell at his apparatus precision.
