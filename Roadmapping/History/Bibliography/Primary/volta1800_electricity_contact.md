# On the Electricity excited by the mere Contact of conducting Substances of different kinds

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR C's QA pass.*

Volta's announcement of the "pile" — alternating disks of zinc and silver separated by brine-soaked cardboard — that produces a sustained electric current. The first reliable source of continuous current, replacing the static-discharge regime of Leyden jars and frictional electrostatics. Published as a letter to Sir Joseph Banks, then-President of the Royal Society. The pile enables every electromagnetism experiment that follows — Ørsted (1820), Faraday (1831), the lot — and is the operational birth of *current* electricity as distinct from static electricity.
