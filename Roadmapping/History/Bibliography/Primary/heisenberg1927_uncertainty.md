# Über den anschaulichen Inhalt der quantentheoretischen Kinematik und Mechanik

*Skeleton bib note.* Heisenberg's uncertainty paper. Argues — via a gamma-ray microscope thought-experiment — that position and momentum cannot both be simultaneously well-defined to arbitrary precision: $\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \gtrsim \hbar$. The Robertson–Schrödinger generalisation $\Delta A \cdot \Delta B \geq \frac{1}{2}|\langle[A,B]\rangle|$ comes in 1929–30. Foundational for the Copenhagen interpretation Bohr crystallises in his Como lecture (September 1927).
