# Relativity in the Global Positioning System

*Skeleton bib note.* Ashby's definitive *Living Reviews* article on all the relativistic effects entering GPS — SR time dilation (satellite velocity gives $-7\,\mu$s/day), GR gravitational time dilation (satellite altitude gives $+45\,\mu$s/day), Sagnac (Earth rotation during signal transit), Shapiro delay (light propagation in curved spacetime), and higher-order corrections. The canonical reference for [[07_PNT_GPS_SLR_QKD|Chapter 7's]] GPS section. **Open access** (Living Reviews).
