# Space-Time Approach to Quantum Electrodynamics

*Skeleton bib note.* Feynman's diagrammatic / path-integral QED. Introduces the spacetime *Feynman diagrams* as a calculation tool — each diagram corresponds to a term in a perturbation series, with explicit rules for translating it into a Lorentz-invariant integral. The diagrams are intuitive enough for graduate students to compute QED processes without going through the heavy operator algebra of Schwinger or Tomonaga. Companion paper to *Theory of Positrons* (1949) which sets up the positron-as-electron-going-backward-in-time interpretation. The diagrammatic technology recurs in Gill's [[Feynman_Operator_Calculus_Papers|Feynman Operator Calculus]] line of work.
