# Energy Production in Stars (Bethe 1939)

*Skeleton bib note.* Bethe's systematic enumeration of nuclear-fusion reaction chains that could power stars. Identifies the pp-chain (dominant in cool low-mass stars) and the CNO cycle (dominant in hot high-mass stars). Combined with the contemporaneous von Weizsäcker work, settles the stellar-fusion mechanism. Bethe receives the 1967 Nobel for this work. The rate-limiting first step of the pp-chain — $p + p \to d + e^+ + \nu_e$ — is governed by the [[fermi1934_beta_decay|weak interaction]] and is why the Sun's lifetime is gigayears rather than seconds.
