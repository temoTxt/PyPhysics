# Possible Existence of a Neutron

*Skeleton bib note.* Chadwick's one-page *Nature* letter announcing the neutron, identified via the energetics of the recoil that a beryllium-derived radiation imparts to nitrogen and other light nuclei. Mass ~1 amu, electrically neutral. Resolves the nuclear-statistics paradox (why nitrogen-14 has integer spin) and provides the building block — alongside the proton — for the entire nuclear-physics programme: nuclear models, beta decay, fission, fusion. The detailed paper follows in *Proc. Roy. Soc. A* later that year.
