# Isotopes and Atomic Weights — Aston

*Skeleton bib note.* Aston's mass-spectrograph measurements establishing that the mass of helium-4 is *less* than the sum of two protons + two neutrons. The "mass defect" $\Delta m = m_{\rm constituents} - m_{\rm nucleus}$ multiplied by $c^2$ gives the *binding energy* of the nucleus. Aston's data motivated [[einstein1905_emc2|Einstein's $E = mc^2$]] as the energy source of stellar luminosity, conjectured shortly after by [[eddington1920_stellar_fusion|Eddington]]. The full binding-energy curve (peak near iron-56) becomes the foundation for fission and fusion.
