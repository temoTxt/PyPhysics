# On Faraday's Lines of Force

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

Maxwell's first paper translating Faraday's qualitative "lines of force" ontology into mathematics — using an analogy with incompressible-fluid streamlines to give a velocity field whose divergence and curl reproduce the magnetostatic and electrostatic field equations. Crucial as the conceptual bridge between Faraday's experimental work (Ch 1) and the 1865 *Dynamical Theory* (the load-bearing anchor for the dual framework). The "lines of force" survive intact in Gill's dual formulation — they're the integral curves of the same E and B fields, just parameterised by proper time τ where standard Maxwell parameterises by observer time t.
