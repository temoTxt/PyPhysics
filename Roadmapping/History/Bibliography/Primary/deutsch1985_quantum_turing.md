# Quantum theory, the Church–Turing principle and the universal quantum computer

*Skeleton bib note.* Deutsch's formalisation of the *universal quantum computer*: a quantum Turing machine that can simulate any other quantum computation with at most polynomial overhead. Proves that such a machine can compute, in polynomial time, certain functions that no classical Turing machine can. The genesis of *quantum complexity theory*.
