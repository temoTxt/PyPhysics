# A Dynamical Theory of the Electromagnetic Field

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

The canonical Maxwell paper. Drops the mechanical-aether scaffolding of *On Physical Lines of Force* and presents twenty coupled differential equations governing E, B, charge, current, and potentials. Shows that transverse EM waves propagate at $c = 1/\sqrt{\mu_0 \varepsilon_0}$, identifying light as an electromagnetic phenomenon. **Load-bearing for this campaign.** The verification campaign ([[Two_Mathematically_Equivalent_Versions_of_Maxwells_Equations]]) anchors the dual-theory contrast on this paper: Eq.
