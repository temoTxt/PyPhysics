# Shor's algorithm for integer factoring

*Skeleton bib note.* Shor's polynomial-time quantum algorithm for factoring large integers (and for the discrete-logarithm problem). Reduces RSA-type cryptography to a problem solvable on a quantum computer in polynomial time, vs. exponential on a classical computer. The *killer-app* result that drove the modern QC field. Implementations: factored 15 (2001 NMR, IBM), 21 (2012 photonic), 15 again on superconducting (2019 Google), gradually growing.
