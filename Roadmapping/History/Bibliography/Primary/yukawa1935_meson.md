# On the Interaction of Elementary Particles. I

*Skeleton bib note.* Yukawa's prediction of a *meson* — a particle exchanged between nucleons that mediates the strong nuclear force — with mass set by the range of the force, $m c^2 \sim \hbar c / R \sim 100$ MeV. Predates pion discovery (Powell et al. 1947) by 12 years; Yukawa receives the 1949 Nobel after the pion is found. Placed in Ch 5's bibliography (rather than Ch 4) because its experimental confirmation and theoretical impact crystallise in the 1948–55 window. The strong-interaction thread sits mostly outside the dual-framework's published scope.
