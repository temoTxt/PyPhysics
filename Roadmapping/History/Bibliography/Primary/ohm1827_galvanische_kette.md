# Die galvanische Kette, mathematisch bearbeitet

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR C's QA pass.*

Ohm's monograph deriving (and experimentally verifying) $V = IR$ — the linear relation between current and potential difference for metallic conductors. Written as a deliberate analogy with Fourier's heat conduction (Ohm corresponded extensively with Fourier's work): just as heat flux scales with the temperature gradient, electric current scales with the potential gradient. Initial reception in Germany was hostile — Ohm was forced to resign from his professorship — but recognition came from the Royal Society (Copley Medal 1841) and the law became foundational for the telegraph industry of the 1840s–50s.
