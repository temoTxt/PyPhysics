# The Transistor, A Semi-Conductor Triode

*Skeleton bib note.* Bardeen and Brattain's point-contact transistor announced from Bell Labs in late December 1947 (published 1948). The first practical solid-state amplifier. Shockley's junction transistor (1949) is more manufacturable; together they win the 1956 Nobel. The transistor's existence rests on Bloch (1928) band theory + Wilson (1931) semiconductor classification + the deliberate doping of germanium and silicon developed during WWII radar work. The transistor revolution of the 1950s–60s drives Moore's-law-style miniaturisation; the chapter's narrative deliberately ends in 1965 just as integrated circuits begin appearing.
