# The Positive Electron

*Skeleton bib note.* Anderson's announcement of the *positron* — observed in cosmic-ray cloud-chamber tracks at Caltech. A particle with the electron's mass but opposite charge, curving the wrong way in a magnetic field. Vindicates Dirac's hole-theory prediction (1930–31) within two years. The first observed example of *antimatter*. In the dual framework: antimatter is naturally framed via Santilli's *isodual* mathematics (sign-reversed metric, time, energy), and the dual Dirac equation in [[Dual_Relativistic_Quantum_Mechanics_I]] gives an isodual companion equation whose solutions correspond to positrons; the standard Dirac negative-energy-hole reinterpretation is replaced by the isodual reinterpretation. **Animated in `hist_positron_isodual.py`.**
