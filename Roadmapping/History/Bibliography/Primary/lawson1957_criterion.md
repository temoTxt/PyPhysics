# Lawson criterion

*Skeleton bib note.* Lawson's calculation of the minimum confinement time $\tau$, particle density $n$, and temperature $T$ required for a thermonuclear plasma to *produce* more energy by fusion than is required to heat it. Originally written in classified UK fusion research (Harwell), declassified and published 1957. The *Lawson criterion* $n\tau \gtrsim 10^{20}\,\text{m}^{-3}\cdot\text{s}$ at $T \sim 10\,\text{keV}$ (deuterium-tritium burn) becomes the engineering target for every confinement approach: magnetic confinement (tokamaks, stellarators), inertial confinement (NIF), and modern alternatives (Z-pinch, magnetised target). The *triple product* $nT\tau$ is the modern figure of merit.
