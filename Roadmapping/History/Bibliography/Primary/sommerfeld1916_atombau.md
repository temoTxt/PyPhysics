# Zur Quantentheorie der Spektrallinien

*Skeleton bib note.* Sommerfeld's relativistic extension of the Bohr model: elliptical orbits + special-relativistic correction give the fine structure of hydrogen, with the famous formula $E_{n,k} = -\frac{R}{n^2}\left[1 + \frac{\alpha^2}{n}\left(\frac{1}{k} - \frac{3}{4n}\right) + \dots\right]$. The first appearance of the fine-structure constant $\alpha = e^2/(\hbar c) \approx 1/137$ in spectroscopy. The same algebra survives, in cleaner form, in [[Dual_Relativistic_Quantum_Mechanics_I|DRQM I]]'s dual-Dirac fine structure (the Foundations II classical-Hamiltonian rebranding makes the dual-framework version of fine structure direct).
