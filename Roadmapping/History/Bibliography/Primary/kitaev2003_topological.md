# Fault-tolerant quantum computation by anyons (Kitaev)

*Skeleton bib note.* Kitaev's proposal for *topological* quantum computing: encode qubits in the topological state of non-Abelian anyons (quasiparticles in 2D systems with fractional statistics). Topological protection makes the qubits inherently robust to local noise. Implementations are speculative — Majorana zero modes in semiconductor-superconductor heterostructures have shown candidate signatures but no confirmed topological qubit operations as of 2023. `#gill-silent`.
