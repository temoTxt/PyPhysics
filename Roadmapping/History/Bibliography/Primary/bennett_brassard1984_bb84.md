# BB84 — Bennett & Brassard quantum key distribution protocol

*Skeleton bib note.* The foundational quantum-cryptography protocol. Alice sends polarised photons in random basis (rectilinear or diagonal); Bob measures in random basis; classical sifting + privacy amplification. Security follows from the no-cloning theorem: an eavesdropper cannot reproduce unknown quantum states without introducing detectable errors. Conference proceedings only; widely cited but never published in a journal.
