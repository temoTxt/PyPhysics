# The Liquefaction of Helium

*Skeleton bib note.* Onnes's announcement of the liquefaction of helium at 4.2 K at his cryogenic laboratory in Leiden. The technical prerequisite for [[onnes1911_superconductivity|superconductivity (1911)]] and for the entire low-temperature solid-state physics that follows. `#gill-silent` — pure thermodynamics-of-cryogenics.
