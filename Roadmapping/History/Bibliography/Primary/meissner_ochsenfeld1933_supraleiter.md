# Ein neuer Effekt bei Eintritt der Supraleitfähigkeit

*Skeleton bib note.* The Meissner–Ochsenfeld effect: a superconductor cooled in a magnetic field *expels* the field as it crosses the critical temperature. Distinguishes superconductivity from "perfect conductivity" — a hypothetical zero-resistance metal would *trap* the field, not expel it. Establishes superconductivity as a fundamentally distinct thermodynamic phase. London brothers' 1935 phenomenological equations capture the Meissner expulsion via $\nabla \times \mathbf{j} = -(n_s e^2/m c) \mathbf{B}$, and the microscopic BCS theory (1957, [[05_QED_renormalization_solid_state_1948_1965|Chapter 5]]) finally explains everything.
