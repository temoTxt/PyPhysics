# Electron spin — Uhlenbeck and Goudsmit's *Naturwissenschaften* note

*Skeleton bib note.* Uhlenbeck and Goudsmit, then graduate students under Ehrenfest at Leiden, identify [[pauli1925_exclusion|Pauli's "Zweideutigkeit"]] (two-valuedness) — the fourth atomic quantum number with no classical analog — as an *intrinsic angular momentum* of the electron with magnitude $\hbar/2$ and g-factor $g = 2$. The g-factor anomaly (classical orbital motion gives $g = 1$) has no explanation in matrix or wave mechanics and remains a postulate for three years until [[dirac1928_electron|Dirac 1928]] derives $g = 2$ as a prediction of the 4-spinor structure.
