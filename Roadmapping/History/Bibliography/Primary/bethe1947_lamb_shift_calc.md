# The Electromagnetic Shift of Energy Levels

*Skeleton bib note.* Bethe's non-relativistic estimate of the Lamb shift, written on a train back from the Shelter Island conference in June 1947 where Lamb had announced the experiment. Uses an *ad hoc* mass-renormalisation cutoff at $E_{\max} = m_e c^2$ to estimate the radiative shift at ~1040 MHz, in agreement with Lamb's ~1000 MHz measurement. The first successful renormalisation calculation in physics — even though Bethe knew it was provisional (the full relativistic version came over the next 18 months from Schwinger, Tomonaga, Feynman). The non-relativistic logic of Bethe's calculation transfers transparently to the dual-framework QED of [[FOUNDATIONS_FOR_QED_I_MATHEMATICAL]].
