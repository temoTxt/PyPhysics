# Intensitas vis magneticae terrestris ad mensuram absolutam revocata

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR C's QA pass.*

Gauss's introduction of *absolute* units for magnetic field strength — expressing the Earth's magnetic field in cm, g, s alone (cgs-Gaussian units), rather than in arbitrary instrument-specific units. The decisive methodological move: combining swinging-magnetometer timing with a coil-deflection experiment to disentangle the magnetic dipole moment from the field strength. This is the conceptual precursor to the SI dimensional system. Gauss and Weber's joint Magnetic Union (Magnetischer Verein, founded 1834) coordinated simultaneous magnetic observations across European observatories using these absolute units.

`#gill-silent` — pure metrology; sets the stage for *every* later quantitative electromagnetism measurement.
