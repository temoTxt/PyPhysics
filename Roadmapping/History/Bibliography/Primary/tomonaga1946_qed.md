# On a Relativistically Invariant Formulation of the Quantum Theory of Wave Fields

*Skeleton bib note.* Tomonaga's covariant formulation of QED, derived independently in Japan during WWII isolation (published 1946 in *Progress of Theoretical Physics* and reaching the West only after the war). Introduces the *interaction picture* and the integration of dynamics over spacelike hypersurfaces. Mathematically equivalent to Schwinger's covariant formulation (independently developed at Harvard) and to Feynman's path-integral / diagrammatic approach. The three approaches share the 1965 Nobel Prize.
