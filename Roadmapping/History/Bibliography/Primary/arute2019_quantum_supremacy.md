# Quantum supremacy using a programmable superconducting processor

*Skeleton bib note.* Google's 53-qubit Sycamore processor performs a random-circuit-sampling computation in ~200 seconds that the team estimates would take a classical supercomputer ~10,000 years. The first claimed demonstration of "quantum supremacy" (later renamed "quantum advantage" for sensitivity reasons). Subsequent IBM analysis argued the classical time was overestimated; Sycamore-class advantage is contested at this circuit depth. Nevertheless: a milestone for superconducting-qubit hardware.
