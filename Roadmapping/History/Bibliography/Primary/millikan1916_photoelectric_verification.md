# A Direct Photoelectric Determination of Planck's 'h'

*Skeleton bib note.* Millikan's painstaking 10-year experimental verification of Einstein's 1905 photoelectric equation $E_k = h\nu - W$. Despite Millikan himself being initially skeptical of the light-quantum hypothesis, his stopping-voltage-vs-frequency plots reproduce the linear relation exactly, with the slope yielding $h$ to ~1% accuracy — independently confirming Planck's blackbody value. The decisive experiment legitimising the quantum hypothesis as physical, not just calculational; Einstein's 1921 Nobel Prize cites the photoelectric paper, vindicated by Millikan.
