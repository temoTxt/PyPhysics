# Further Experiments with Liquid Helium. C. On the Change of Electric Resistance…

*Skeleton bib note.* Onnes's discovery of *superconductivity* in mercury at 4.19 K — electrical resistance drops abruptly to zero below the critical temperature. Unexplained for 46 years until BCS theory (1957). The first major physical phenomenon visible only in the cryogenic regime; opens the long line that runs through the Meissner effect (1933), London equations (1935), BCS (1957), Josephson junctions (1962), and modern circuit QED for quantum computing. Treated at length in [[05_QED_renormalization_solid_state_1948_1965|Chapter 5]] when the microscopic mechanism is finally given.
