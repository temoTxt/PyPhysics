# La théorie électromagnétique de Maxwell et son application aux corps mouvants

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

Lorentz's first systematic electron theory: Maxwell's equations + a discrete electron carrier of charge as the source of currents and dielectric polarisation. Reformulates Maxwell-in-matter in terms of microscopic electrons + an aether for the macroscopic field. The Lorentz transformations between aether-rest frame and moving-frame appear in subsequent papers (1895, 1899, 1904) and are introduced as a mathematical device for making the calculations work, not yet as a kinematic principle (that's Einstein 1905). In the dual framework, the Lorentz transformations of *time* survive intact (proper time replaces them with the $b/c$ rescaling), but the Lorentz contraction of *length* is *not* required — see [[Two_Mathematically_Equivalent_Versions_of_Maxwells_Equations#Eq. (10) — Proper-time boosts of position, velocity, acceleration]].
