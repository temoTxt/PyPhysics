# Satellite-to-Ground QKD — Micius

*Skeleton bib note.* The Chinese Micius satellite (launched 2016) achieves BB84 quantum key distribution between satellite and ground stations at ~kHz key rate over ~1200 km links. The first demonstration of intercontinental QKD via satellite relay. Relevant to Ch 7's QKD section as the current state-of-the-art demonstration; relevant to Ch 8 (quantum-computing open questions) as the natural testbed for `#speculative` extensions involving Gill's KS-Hilbert space.
