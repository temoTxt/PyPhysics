# Theory of Superconductivity (BCS)

*Skeleton bib note.* The microscopic theory of superconductivity, 46 years after [[onnes1911_superconductivity|Onnes's discovery]]. Cooper pairs: electrons near the Fermi surface form bound pairs via phonon exchange; the ground state of the pairs is separated from excitations by a gap $\Delta$. The Cooper-pair condensate gives zero resistance ($\rho = 0$ for currents below the critical value) and the Meissner effect ($\nabla\times\mathbf{j} = -(n_s e^2/mc)\mathbf{B}$). Nobel 1972. Bardeen is the only person to win two physics Nobels (1956 for the transistor + 1972 for BCS). `#gill-silent`; the chapter [[08_quantum_computing_open_questions|Chapter 8 on quantum computing]] proposes a `#speculative` connection between Gill's effective photon mass and circuit QED coherence times in superconducting qubits.
