# Über den Zusammenhang des Abschlusses der Elektronengruppen im Atom mit der Komplexstruktur der Spektren

*Skeleton bib note.* Pauli's exclusion principle: no two electrons in an atom can occupy the same four-quantum-number state $(n, \ell, m, m_s)$. Empirically motivated by the atomic shell structure and the closing of noble-gas configurations. The fourth quantum number — soon identified as spin by Uhlenbeck and Goudsmit — has *no classical analog*; Pauli initially calls it "Zweideutigkeit" (two-valuedness). The exclusion principle, married to Schrödinger + Dirac, gives the entire periodic table; in solid-state ([[bloch1928_kristall|Bloch 1928]]) it gives the Fermi distribution and ultimately band theory.
