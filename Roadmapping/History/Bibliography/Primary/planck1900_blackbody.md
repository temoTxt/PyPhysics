# Zur Theorie des Gesetzes der Energieverteilung im Normalspectrum

*Skeleton bib note.* Planck's December 1900 derivation of the blackbody radiation formula via the quantisation hypothesis $E = nh\nu$. Introduced *ad hoc* to fit the experimental Lummer–Pringsheim curve and reconcile the Wien (high-frequency) and Rayleigh–Jeans (low-frequency) limits. Planck himself treats $h$ as a calculational artefact for the better part of a decade — the *interpretation* of quantisation as physically real only solidifies after Einstein's 1905 photoelectric paper.
