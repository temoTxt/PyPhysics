# Gravitation (MTW)

*Skeleton bib note.* The encyclopedic graduate textbook on general relativity. Two-track structure: a basic-track (~500 pages) accessible to advanced undergraduates and an advanced-track (~1000 pages) for graduate students. Covers Schwarzschild, Mercury perihelion, GR tests, black holes, gravitational waves, cosmology. **In copyright** (Freeman 1973 / Princeton reprint 2017); kept local only.
