# Tokamaks (Wesson)

*Skeleton bib note.* The standard graduate textbook on tokamak fusion physics. Comprehensive coverage of MHD equilibrium, stability, transport, heating, current drive, and the experimental progress from T-3 (1958) through JET, JT-60, and looking forward to ITER. **In copyright** (OUP 2011); kept local only.
