# QED and the Men Who Made It

*Skeleton bib note.* Schweber's definitive history of QED renormalisation, focused on the four 1965-Nobel-share laureates: Dyson, Feynman, Schwinger, Tomonaga. Particularly strong on the 1947 Shelter Island conference where Lamb announced the experiment that triggered everything; the 1948 Pocono conference where Schwinger lectured for 8 hours on his covariant formulation; and the 1949 Old Stone conference where Dyson presented his equivalence proof. **In copyright** (Princeton 1994); kept local only.
