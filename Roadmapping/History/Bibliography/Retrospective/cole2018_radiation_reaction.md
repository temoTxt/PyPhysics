# Experimental Evidence of Radiation Reaction in the Collision of a High-Intensity Laser Pulse with a Laser-Wakefield Accelerated Electron Beam

*Skeleton bib note generated by `scaffold_bib_note.py`, then hand-edited.*
First of three 2018 radiation-reaction experiments under evaluation in [issue #43](https://github.com/temoTxt/PyPhysics/issues/43).
Geometry: ~550 MeV laser-wakefield-accelerated electron beam at the Astra-Gemini facility (Central Laser Facility, Rutherford Appleton Laboratory), counter-propagating against a ~10²¹ W/cm² laser pulse (peak `a_0 = 24.7 ± 0.7` nominal, effective `a_0 ≈ 12 ± 1` after spatio-temporal jitter); quantum nonlinearity parameter `η ≈ 0.07`. Observables: post-collision electron energy spectrum + simultaneous γ-ray spectrum on CsI scintillator. Paper preferred a quantum (stochastic) radiation-reaction model over the classical Landau–Lifshitz form at the ~1σ level, via the ratio `R = a_0(γ-ray)/a_0(electron)` of the laser amplitudes inferred independently from the two diagnostics.
