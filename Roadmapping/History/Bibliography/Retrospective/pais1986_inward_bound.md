# Inward Bound

*Skeleton bib note.* Pais's history of particle physics from 1895 to the early 1980s. Companion to [[pais1982_subtle_is_the_lord|*Subtle Is the Lord*]] (Einstein) but focused on the elementary-particle programme: cosmic rays, mesons, V-particles, strange particles, the Eightfold Way, quarks, gauge theories. Particularly load-bearing for Ch 5's coverage of the parity-violation discovery (Wu, Garwin–Lederman) and the broader weak-interaction context. **In copyright** (OUP 1986); kept local only.
