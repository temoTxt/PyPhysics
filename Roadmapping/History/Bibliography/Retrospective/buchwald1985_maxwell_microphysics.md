# From Maxwell to Microphysics

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

Buchwald's history of the post-Maxwell generation: how British "Maxwellians" (Heaviside, Lodge, FitzGerald, Larmor) and Continental physicists (Hertz, Helmholtz, Boltzmann) interpreted, extended, and disagreed about Maxwell's equations between 1873 and 1900. Detailed treatment of the displacement-current debates, the move from quaternion to vector notation, the reception of Hertz's experiments, and the path from Maxwell-the-field-theorist to Lorentz-the-electron-theorist. Essential for Ch 2's narrative of how the British field-tradition and the Continental action-at-a-distance tradition reconciled after Hertz. **In copyright** (Chicago 1985); kept local only.
