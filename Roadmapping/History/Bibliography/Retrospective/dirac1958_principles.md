# The Principles of Quantum Mechanics (4th ed.)

*Skeleton bib note.* Dirac's own textbook — the first systematic exposition of quantum mechanics in bra-ket notation that he invented. Classifies as retrospective only because the 1st edition is 1930 (slightly outside the post-Dirac-equation cleanup) and the canonical 4th edition (1958) reflects three decades of refinement. The book where Dirac introduces the delta function, the transformation theory, and the algebraic formulation that becomes standard. Particularly relevant for Ch 4: Ch XI of *Principles* gives Dirac's own clean exposition of the relativistic theory of the electron. **In copyright** (OUP 1958); kept local only.
