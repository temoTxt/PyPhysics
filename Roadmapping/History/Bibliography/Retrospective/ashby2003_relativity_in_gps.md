# Relativity in the Global Positioning System (Ashby 2003)

*Skeleton bib note.* The canonical reference for the relativistic clock corrections applied to the GPS satellite constellation. Covers (a) the Schwarzschild geodesic in post-Newtonian expansion to order `1/c²`, (b) the headline `+38.6 μs/day` net offset between satellite and ground clocks (gravitational redshift minus velocity dilation), (c) the eccentricity correction `Δt = −(2/c²)√(GMa) · e · sin E` with `≤ 46 ns` periodic amplitude at `e = 0.02`, (d) the Sagnac effect in the rotating ECEF frame with `≤ 133 ns` path-dependent contribution, (e) the Shapiro propagation delay through Earth's gravitational well at the `≤ 19 ps` level, and (f) the J₂-induced secular and periodic corrections. The article also documents the operational frequency offset `Δf/f = −4.4647 × 10⁻¹⁰` programmed into satellite clocks before launch, taking the fundamental from `10.23 MHz` to `10.229 999 995 43 MHz`. **Open-access Living Reviews article**; PDF acquisition pending.
