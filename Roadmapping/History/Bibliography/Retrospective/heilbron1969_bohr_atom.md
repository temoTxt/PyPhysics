# The Genesis of the Bohr Atom

*Skeleton bib note.* Heilbron and Kuhn's archival study of Bohr's 1912–1913 path to the atomic model: not the clean inductive sequence ("Rydberg + Rutherford → Bohr postulates") of the textbook version, but a tangled progression involving Bohr's Manchester sojourn, his exposure to Nicholson's molecular-spectra ideas, and his eventual realisation that the Balmer formula was the key data point. **In copyright** (1969); kept local only.
