# The Physics of Inertial Fusion (Atzeni & Meyer-ter-Vehn)

*Skeleton bib note.* The standard textbook on inertial-confinement fusion (ICF) — laser-driven implosion of small deuterium-tritium capsules. Comprehensive coverage of hohlraum physics, hydrodynamic instabilities (Rayleigh-Taylor, Richtmyer-Meshkov), capsule design, and the path to ignition that NIF eventually achieved in 2022. **In copyright** (OUP 2004); kept local only.
