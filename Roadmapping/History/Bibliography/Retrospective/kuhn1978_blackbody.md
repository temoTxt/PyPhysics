# Black-Body Theory and the Quantum Discontinuity, 1894-1912

*Skeleton bib note.* Kuhn's controversial reading of Planck's 1900 paper as *not* yet a discontinuous-quantum result — arguing that the genuine quantum-jump revolution comes in 1906–1908 with Einstein and Ehrenfest's rereading of Planck's combinatorial argument. Useful counterweight to the standard "Planck invented the quantum" textbook narrative. **In copyright** (Oxford 1978 / Chicago 1987); kept local only.
