# The Conceptual Development of Quantum Mechanics

*Skeleton bib note.* Jammer's standard history of the old quantum theory and its replacement: Planck → Einstein → Bohr → Sommerfeld → de Broglie → Heisenberg → Schrödinger. Particularly strong on the *conceptual* difficulties of the 1900–1925 period — what people thought a quantum *was*, whether the quantum hypothesis was real or calculational, how the Bohr–Sommerfeld old quantum theory broke down under finer experimental tests (anomalous Zeeman effect, helium spectrum) and forced the 1925 revolution. **In copyright** (McGraw-Hill 1966); kept local only.
