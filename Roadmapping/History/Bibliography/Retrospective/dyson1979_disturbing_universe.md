# Disturbing the Universe

*Skeleton bib note.* Dyson's autobiography. Ch 5–7 cover the 1947–1949 QED unification: his summer 1948 bus ride during which he saw how to unify Feynman's diagrams with Schwinger and Tomonaga's covariant formulations; his subsequent paper that won the rest of the physics community over to Feynman's diagrammatic approach. Direct primary-source material for the historical narrative of Ch 5 §2.1. **In copyright** (Harper & Row 1979); kept local only.
