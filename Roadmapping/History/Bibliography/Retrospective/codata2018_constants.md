# CODATA 2018 Recommended Values of the Fundamental Physical Constants

*Skeleton bib note generated by `scaffold_bib_note.py`, then hand-edited.*
The CODATA Task Group on Fundamental Constants' 2018 adjustment, published 2021 in *Reviews of Modern Physics*. The canonical source for fundamental-constant values (electron mass, fine-structure constant, Rydberg constant, electron g-factor, etc.) that the `nist.get_constant` tool in [`precision_data_mcp`](../../../../mcp_servers/precision_data_mcp/) exposes via `scipy.constants` (which bundles the CODATA 2018 release).

Per [issue #92 §Resolved-decisions #9](https://github.com/temoTxt/PyPhysics/issues/92), the cite-key convention for periodic-adjustment sources like CODATA uses `<issuing-body><year>_<topic>` (here `codata2018_constants`); the `year` field is the adjustment-data year (2018) rather than the publication year (2021), since downstream verification documents care about *which adjustment* is being cited.

When the umbrella's `scipy` dependency bumps to a CODATA-2022 or later release, a separate bib stub `codata2022_constants` (etc.) is scaffolded alongside this one; the cached `nist.get_constant` records track the adjustment edition in their `source_revision` field per [`REFRESH_POLICY.md`](../../../../mcp_servers/precision_data_mcp/REFRESH_POLICY.md) §6. The previous edition stays cited by older verification documents — citations are never silently re-pointed.
