# The Historical Development of Quantum Theory (6 vols)

*Skeleton bib note.* Mehra and Rechenberg's monumental six-volume history of quantum theory from Planck (1900) through the late 1930s. The most detailed scholarly account in print of the 1925–1928 revolution: Heisenberg's Helgoland breakthrough, the matrix-vs-wave-mechanics debate, the formal equivalence proof, the Dirac equation, and the early reception of antimatter. Vol 5 (Erwin Schrödinger and the Rise of Wave Mechanics) and Vol 6 (The Completion of Quantum Mechanics 1926–1941) most directly relevant. **In copyright** (Springer); kept local only.
