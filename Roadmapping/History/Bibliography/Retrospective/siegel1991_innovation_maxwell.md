# Innovation in Maxwell's Electromagnetic Theory

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR D's QA pass.*

Close historical reading of Maxwell's three foundational papers (1855, 1861–62, 1865) — specifically how the mechanical-aether vortex model of *On Physical Lines of Force* forced the discovery of displacement current. Siegel argues, contra the standard "Maxwell guessed the right answer for the wrong reason" reading, that the mechanical model was a genuine *productive* tool, not scaffolding to be discarded. Useful counterweight to the cleaner mathematical-physics reading of Maxwell that dominates physics textbooks. **In copyright** (Cambridge 1991); kept local only.
