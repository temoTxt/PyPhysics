# The Quantum Theory of Fields, Vol I: Foundations

*Skeleton bib note.* Weinberg's three-volume QFT treatise (Vols I 1995, II 1996, III 2000). The standard graduate reference for QFT foundations from a *bottom-up* perspective: starting from Lorentz invariance + unitarity + cluster decomposition and deriving QFT as the unique consistent framework. Cited throughout the verification campaign as the standard-equation reference for relativistic-QM and QFT comparisons. Chapter 1's historical introduction is particularly useful for Ch 4's narrative of the matrix/wave/Dirac convergence. **In copyright** (CUP 1995); kept local only.
