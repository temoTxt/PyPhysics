*Skeleton bib note.* Krantz et al.'s 100-page tutorial on superconducting-qubit hardware: transmon design, readout, single- and two-qubit gates, coherence sources and limits, the role of the Josephson-junction nonlinearity. The de facto modern reference for circuit-QED engineering. **arXiv open access.**
