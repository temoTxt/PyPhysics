# Michael Faraday: A Biography

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR C's QA pass.*

Williams's standard scholarly biography, drawing heavily on Faraday's surviving lab notebooks at the Royal Institution. Particularly strong on the 1820s–30s electromagnetism work and on Faraday's theological framework (he was a devout Sandemanian) and how that shaped his commitment to "lines of force" as a physically real continuous-field ontology — in contrast to Continental action-at-a-distance theorists (Ampère, Weber, Neumann). The book is **in copyright** (Basic Books 1965); PDF kept local only under fair use, with only quoted passages and verified citations cross-referenced into the chapter.

Used in Ch 1 to motivate the field-line ontology that becomes load-bearing in Maxwell (Ch 2) and survives into Gill's framework via the dual EM field tensors.
