# Introduction to Superconductivity

*Skeleton bib note.* Tinkham's graduate textbook on superconductivity — the standard reference, covering the London phenomenology, BCS microscopic theory, Ginzburg–Landau, type-I/type-II superconductors, Josephson effects, the high-$T_c$ cuprates, and (in the 2nd ed.) flux quantisation and the relation to mesoscopic physics. Particularly load-bearing for [[08_quantum_computing_open_questions|Chapter 8's]] discussion of superconducting-qubit coherence. **In copyright** (McGraw-Hill 1996, Dover 2004); kept local only.
