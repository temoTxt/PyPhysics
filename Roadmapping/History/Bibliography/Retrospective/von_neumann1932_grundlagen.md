# Mathematische Grundlagen der Quantenmechanik

*Skeleton bib note.* Von Neumann's axiomatisation of quantum mechanics on Hilbert space. Proves the equivalence of matrix and wave mechanics rigorously. Introduces the projection-valued measure for observables, the trace formula for expectation values, and the density-matrix formalism for statistical ensembles. The mathematical scaffolding on which all subsequent foundations work — including [[FOUNDATIONS_FOR_QED_I_MATHEMATICAL|Gill's *Foundations I: Mathematical*]] — rests. **English translation 1955 (Princeton).** Classified as retrospective because we cite it for its *systematic* role, not for its discoveries (which are mostly Heisenberg's and Schrödinger's).
