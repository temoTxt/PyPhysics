# Quantum Computing in the NISQ era and beyond

*Skeleton bib note.* Preskill's coining of *NISQ* (Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum) — the regime of 50-1000 qubits without fault tolerance. Reviews what NISQ devices can and can't do; what near-term algorithms (variational quantum eigensolver, QAOA) might achieve advantage; and what the path to fault-tolerant quantum computing looks like. Standard modern reference for the current QC landscape. **arXiv open access.**
