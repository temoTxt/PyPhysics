# Classical Electrodynamics (2nd ed.)

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during the Electromagnetism campaign's PR A QA pass.*

The Gaussian-units edition of Jackson's graduate textbook. **Used in the Electromagnetism / Jackson campaign** (issue [#42](https://github.com/temoTxt/PyPhysics/issues/42)) as the canonical CGS reference; the 3rd edition ([[jackson1998_classical_electrodynamics]]) is the SI reference for Chs. 1–10. Per [§4 of the campaign plan](../../../../.dev/tasks/42-electromagnetism-jackson-proper-time.md#4-unit-system-handling), the 2nd edition is the primary source for any problem where the 3rd edition has switched to SI but the original Gaussian derivation is more pedagogically natural — in practice, all of Chs. 11+ and most of the proper-time-comparison work. **In copyright** (Wiley 1975); kept local only.
