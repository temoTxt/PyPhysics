# Quantum Computation and Quantum Information

*Skeleton bib note.* The standard graduate textbook on quantum computing and quantum information. Comprehensive coverage of qubits, gates, algorithms (Shor, Grover), error correction, fault tolerance, quantum cryptography. Standard reference for both [[07_PNT_GPS_SLR_QKD|Chapter 7's]] QKD section and [[08_quantum_computing_open_questions|Chapter 8's]] open-questions roadmap. **In copyright** (Cambridge 2000); kept local only.
