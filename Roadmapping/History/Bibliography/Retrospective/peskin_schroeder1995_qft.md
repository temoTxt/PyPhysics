# An Introduction to Quantum Field Theory

*Skeleton bib note.* The standard *teaching* textbook for QFT (where Weinberg's vols are the standard *reference*). Walks through Klein–Gordon (Ch 2) and Dirac (Ch 3) with concrete examples; the negative-energy / probability-density problems are discussed explicitly. Cited throughout the verification campaign as the standard reference for QFT computations. **In copyright** (Addison-Wesley 1995); kept local only.
