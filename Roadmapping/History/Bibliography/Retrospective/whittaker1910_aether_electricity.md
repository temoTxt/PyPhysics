# A History of the Theories of Aether and Electricity

*Skeleton bib note. Body to be filled in during PR C's QA pass.*

The classic narrative history of pre-relativistic electrodynamics, written by a working mathematician (Whittaker held the Royal Astronomer of Ireland chair) rather than a historian. Chapters covering 1800–1860 (the Voltaic, Ørsted, Ampère, Faraday, and Kirchhoff eras) are detailed and mathematically literate; the book is the de facto reference for "what people thought they were doing" in 19th-century electromagnetism. Whittaker is famously partisan in his much-later (1953) two-volume edition's treatment of relativity priority, but the 1910 first edition covering the 1800–1860 material is generally trusted. **Public-domain 1910 edition** available on archive.org.
