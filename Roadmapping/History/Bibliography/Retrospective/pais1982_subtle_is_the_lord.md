# Subtle Is the Lord

*Skeleton bib note.* Pais's combined scientific biography of Einstein, written by a colleague (Institute for Advanced Study) with full access to the Einstein archive. The standard reference for the genesis of special relativity, GR, the 1905 papers, and Einstein's later quantum-foundations debates with Bohr. Particularly load-bearing for Ch 3's narrative of the 1905 *annus mirabilis* and the 1915–16 GR completion. **In copyright** (OUP 1982); kept local only.
