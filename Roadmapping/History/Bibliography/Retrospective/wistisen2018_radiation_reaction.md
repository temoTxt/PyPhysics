# Experimental evidence of quantum radiation reaction in aligned crystals

*Skeleton bib note generated by `scaffold_bib_note.py`, then hand-edited.*
Third of three 2018 radiation-reaction experiments under evaluation in [issue #43](https://github.com/temoTxt/PyPhysics/issues/43). Geometry: ~200 GeV electrons channeled through an aligned silicon crystal at CERN SPS; observable is the emitted-photon spectrum. Paper preferred classical Landau–Lifshitz over quantum corrections — opposite tilt from the Cole/Poder Gemini-facility geometry, so the two experiments together highlight a *theoretical-comparator* disagreement that the proper-time framework is a candidate to dissolve or sharpen.
