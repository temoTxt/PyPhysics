# Experimental evidence of quantum radiation reaction in aligned crystals

*Skeleton bib note generated by `scaffold_bib_note.py`, then hand-edited.*
Third of three 2018 radiation-reaction experiments under evaluation in [issue #43](https://github.com/temoTxt/PyPhysics/issues/43). Geometry: 178.2 GeV **positrons** (not electrons; the parent issue text approximated as "~200 GeV electron channeling" but the paper measures positrons) channeled through aligned silicon crystals along the ⟨111⟩ axis at CERN SPS H4 beam line; two crystal thicknesses (3.8 mm and 10.0 mm) are reported. Quantum parameter `χ ≲ 1.4`, the highest of the three 2018 experiments. Observable: single-photon emission spectrum reconstructed via a thin Ta converter foil and downstream magnetic spectrometer (suppresses calorimeter-pile-up that obscured prior channeling measurements). Paper compares four models (CRRM / SCRRM / QRRM / QnoRRM): CRRM (classical LL with radiation reaction) is best in the 3.8 mm thin crystal; QRRM (quantum + radiation reaction) is best in the 10.0 mm thick crystal and overall, with the explicit caveat that QRRM "does not completely describe all the data in this regime." The Gemini-facility geometry's preference for quantum-corrected over classical does not invert here; the more nuanced thin/thick split is the data Wistisen reports.
