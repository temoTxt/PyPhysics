\section{Proper-time canonical Hamiltonian $K$ reduces to non-relativistic kinetic energy}
\label{sec:eq-bs-s3}

Source: \texttt{Mathematica\_Notebooks/Quantum\_Mechanics/BetheSalpeter\_S3.wl}
(38 lines). Companion to \texttt{Bethe\_Salpeter/01\_NonRelHydrogen.md}.
This is the PR~A scaffold result.

\paragraph{Statement.}
The proper-time canonical Hamiltonian
\[
K \;=\; \frac{H_0^2}{2 m c^2} \;+\; \frac{m c^2}{2}, \qquad H_0^2 \;=\; c^2 p^2 + m^2 c^4
\]
satisfies the algebraic identity
\[
\boxed{\,K \;\equiv\; m c^2 \;+\; \frac{p^2}{2 m}\,}
\qquad \wolframcheck{\texttt{FullSimplify} returns $0$.}
\]

\paragraph{Honest scope of this check.}
The identity is an \emph{algebraic} simplification by construction. $K$ is
defined as $(c^2 p^2 + m^2 c^4)/(2 m c^2) + m c^2 / 2$; elementary arithmetic
gives $= p^2/(2m) + m c^2$. Wolfram confirms the arithmetic; it does
\textbf{not} verify physical content.

In particular this notebook does \textbf{not} verify that $K$ reproduces the
relativistic hydrogen spectrum. The actual relativistic kinetic energy
$\sqrt{c^2 p^2 + m^2 c^4} = m c^2 + p^2/(2m) - p^4/(8 m^3 c^2) + \dots$ does
\textbf{not} match $K = m c^2 + p^2/(2m)$ starting at $p^4/(8 m^3 c^2)$. The
framework's relativistic content lives in the dual-Dirac equation
(\,PR~C onward), not in $K$.

The campaign's ``$K$ reduces exactly to non-relativistic kinetic'' claim is
true as algebra and is the load-bearing scaffold for PRs~A--B (which exercise
observables at non-relativistic order only). It is \emph{not} a claim about
relativistic, fine-structure, or higher-order content.
