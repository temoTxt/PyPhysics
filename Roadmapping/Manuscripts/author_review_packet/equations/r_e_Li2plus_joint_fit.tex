\paragraph{Gating structural result.}
The (III.22) closed form $r_e/r_0 = (2 - a_e)/(2(2 + a_e))$ depends on exactly
two inputs: the \emph{free}-electron anomaly $a_e$ and
$r_0 = e^2/(m_e c^2)$. Neither depends on $Z$. The radiating particle is the
same electron at every $Z$, so $r_e/r_0$ is \emph{identical} at $Z=1$ and
$Z=3$.

Contrast the lepton axis (PR~\#70): there $r_\mu/r_0^\mu \neq r_e/r_0^e$ at
$>57{,}000\sigma$ because both $a_\ell$ and $r_0^\ell$ vary with lepton mass.
On the $Z$ axis, neither varies $\Rightarrow$ the cutoff is $Z$-invariant
\emph{by the framework's algebra}. Therefore the universal-cutoff reading
(Z-i) is the framework-correct reading; the $Z$-scaled-cutoff reading (Z-ii)
is not derivable; outcome-matrix Branch~B is ruled out structurally.

\paragraph{Observable \#1 --- bound $g$-factor.}
Dirac 1s binding $g(Z) = (2/3)\bigl[1 + 2\sqrt{1 - (Z\alpha)^2}\bigr]$;
add free anomaly $2 a_e$ from the $Z$-universal cutoff:
\[
g_{\rm bound}(Z) = \tfrac{2}{3}\bigl[1 + 2\sqrt{1 - (Z\alpha)^2}\bigr] + 2 a_e.
\]
At $Z=3$, $Z\alpha = 0.0218921$, $g_{\rm bound}(3) \approx 2.00200$.
