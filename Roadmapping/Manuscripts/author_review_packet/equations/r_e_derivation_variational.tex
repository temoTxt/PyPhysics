\paragraph{Route X structural diagnosis.}
\begin{itemize}[topsep=2pt,itemsep=0pt]
  \item Electron-radius scale ($\hat a \sim 1$, $\hat r \sim 0.5$):
        $\langle T\rangle/(mc^2) \sim 1/(2\alpha^2) \sim 9400$. The
        non-relativistic kinetic-energy expression is invalid here;
        DRQM~I~(III.4) is an expansion valid for $r \gg \hbar/(mc)$ and
        $V_0/(mc^2) \ll 1$, both of which fail at $r \sim r_e$.
  \item Bohr scale ($\hat a \sim 1/\alpha^2$, $\hat r \sim 0.5$): cutoff is
        invisible (cutoff $\sim r_0$, trial concentrated at
        $a_B = r_0/\alpha^2$, ratio $\sim \alpha^2 \sim 5\times 10^{-5}$).
        $dE/d\hat r \sim \alpha^6 \sim 10^{-13}$ --- closure cannot pin
        $\hat r$.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Conclusion (Route X).} Closure condition~\#7 with the published
expanded $K_D$ and an exponential NR trial \emph{cannot} determine $\hat r$
first-principles. Resolution requires either (i) the framework supplying an
explicit $\Delta E_{\rm SE}^{\rm framework}(r_e)$ (currently unspecified in
DRQM~I) or (ii) re-deriving from the un-expanded full Dirac equation under a
radial cutoff regulator.
