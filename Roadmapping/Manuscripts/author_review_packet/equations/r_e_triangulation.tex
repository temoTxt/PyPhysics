\section{Empirical joint-fit triangulation of $r_e/r_0$}
\label{sec:eq-triangulation}

Source: \texttt{Mathematica\_Notebooks/Quantum\_Mechanics/r\_e\_triangulation.wl}
(224 lines, closes issue~\#61). Companion to
\texttt{Equation\_Verification/FINDINGS\_for\_author\_review.md} Finding~2.

\paragraph{Setup.}
The cutoff formula from DRQM~I Eq.~(III.21)--(III.22) reads
\[
g_r(x) \;=\; 2\left[1 - \frac{4}{2x + 1}\right], \qquad x \equiv r/r_0,
\]
with measured electron $g$-factor $g_s^{\rm meas} = -2.00231930436256$
(CODATA-2018, $\sigma \approx 3.5\times 10^{-13}$).

\paragraph{Observable spec.}
Per \texttt{Bethe\_Salpeter/10\_CrossComparison.md} \S2, each of the six
$g_s$-dependent observables enters the prediction as
$\mathrm{anchor}_i \cdot (g_r(x)/g_s^{\rm meas})^{n_i}$ with
$n_i \in \{1,2\}$. The six rows are: electron $g_s$,
H~$2P_{3/2}{-}2P_{1/2}$, H~1S hyperfine, He~$^3P_0{-}^3P_1$,
positronium ortho--para, muonium hyperfine.

\paragraph{Two weighting passes (substantive AI choice).}
\begin{align*}
\text{Pass A (measurement-}\sigma\text{ only):}\quad
  &\chi^2_A(r) = \sum_i \left(\frac{p_i(r) - m_i}{\sigma_i^{\rm meas}}\right)^2,\\
\text{Pass B (meas-}\sigma\text{ + framework floor):}\quad
  &\chi^2_B(r) = \sum_i \left(\frac{p_i(r) - m_i}{\sqrt{(\sigma_i^{\rm meas})^2 + \sigma_i^{\rm floor,2}}}\right)^2,
\end{align*}
where $\sigma_i^{\rm floor} = |{\rm anchor}_i - m_i|$ captures the framework's
Bethe-estimate / sub-leading-QED residual at branch~(c).

\paragraph{Triangulated value.}
\[
\boxed{\;
  \frac{r_e}{r_0} \;=\; 0.4994205099128317
  \quad\pm\quad 2.50 \times 10^{-13}
\;}
\]
This matches branch~(c) ($0.4994205099128318$) to 16 significant figures.

\paragraph{Per-observable residual diagnostics at Pass~B (= branch (c)).}
All non-zero residuals are documented framework-precision-floor effects, not
$r$-dependent tensions:
\begin{itemize}[topsep=2pt,itemsep=0pt]
  \item H~$2P_{3/2}{-}2P_{1/2}$: $-7.13$~MHz (Bethe-estimate floor;
        \texttt{03\_FineStructure.md})
  \item H~1S hyperfine: $-0.366$~MHz (sub-leading QED;
        \texttt{06\_Hyperfine.md})
  \item He~$^3P_0{-}^3P_1$: $-0.002$~MHz (kHz residual;
        \texttt{09\_HeliumExcited.md})
  \item Muonium hyperfine: $+0.097$~MHz (sub-leading QED)
  \item Electron $g_s$ and positronium ortho--para match by construction
        at branch~(c).
\end{itemize}
Stretched-fit flag at Pass~B: \textbf{none}.

\paragraph{Crocco compliance.}
Arithmetic (formula evaluation, \texttt{FindMinimum}, Hessian, residual table)
is \emph{pragmatic} AI. The objective-function choice ($\sigma$ model),
weighting interpretation, the Pass~A vs.~Pass~B contrast, and the conclusion
that branch~(c) is the joint-best-fit are \emph{substantive} AI.

\paragraph{Reading.}
The empirical triangulation, under honest weighting, returns branch~(c). The
natural framing: the published $r_e$ is an \emph{initial-value} result from a
uni-observable numerical search against $g_s$ alone; the triangulated value is
a \emph{refinement calculation} on top of it, using all six $g_s$-dependent
observables as joint constraints. Issue~\#54's first-principles derivation, if
pursued, is a potential further refinement.
