\paragraph{Cell~2 --- Framework prediction at $Z=3$ under reading (Z-i).}
Fine structure is spin--orbit $\Rightarrow$ linear in $g_s/(-2)$, $n_{\rm FS}=1$:
\[
\Delta E_{\rm FS}^{\rm fw}(Z) = \frac{g_s}{-2}\,\Delta E_{\rm leading\,Dirac}(Z),
\qquad g_s = -2.00231930\ldots
\]
At $Z=3$ under (Z-i) (universal cutoff):
\[
\boxed{\;
  \Delta E_{\rm FS}^{\rm fw}(Z=3) = 887{,}920\;\text{MHz} \approx 887.92\;\text{GHz}.
\;}
\]
Leading Dirac $886{,}892$ + anomalous offset $+1028$~MHz. Infinite-nuclear-mass;
$^7$Li reduced-mass + recoil refinement $\approx -0.02\%$ ($-200$~MHz), within
the Bethe-estimate precision floor.

(Z-ii) Z-scaled cutoff: not derivable; if no framework-internal $Z$-scaling
emerges, (Z-ii) $\equiv$ (Z-i).

\paragraph{Cell~3 --- Observable \#2 Lamb-shift Z-scaling.}
Weak discriminator: leading log-Bethe is $g_s$-symmetric $\Rightarrow$
$r_e$-independent $\Rightarrow$ (Z-i) $\equiv$ (Z-ii). Reproduction not
endorsement (cf.~\texttt{10\_CrossComparison.md}~\S3). $Z$-scaling
$= Z^4 \cdot [\text{bracket}(Z)/\text{bracket}(1)]$, bracket$(Z) = \ln(1/(Z\alpha)^2) - L_{\rm B}(2S) + 19/30$, with $L_{\rm B}(2S) \approx 2.812$.
