\noindent\textit{Reading the cross-check column:}
the electron $g_s$ entry is the only one carrying an authoritative
independent source (CODATA-2018). The hydrogen 2P fine-structure entry is
ASD-theoretical (Ritz level difference), not a measurement; it differs from
the measured value by $\sim 0.08$~MHz. The remaining four entries are
curated transcriptions of the measurement column from the
\texttt{list\_dirac\_targets} registry (the \texttt{nist} MCP server, PR~\#91
/ issue~\#90), with provenance metadata only --- they restate, not corroborate.
