\paragraph{Total: 28 results across 10 PRs.}
Of these:
\begin{itemize}[topsep=2pt,itemsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{20 unconditional} --- but read this honestly: every one is
        annotated ``formulation-independent,'' ``operator + wavefunction
        identity,'' ``identical to textbook,'' ``inherited from Schr\"odinger
        algebra,'' ``Fermi statistics,'' etc. They are agreement-by-structure:
        the dual-theory framework's prediction \emph{equals} the textbook
        prediction because the framework reduces to textbook QM at the level
        the observable engages.
  \item \textbf{6 at the triangulated $r_e$} (precision $g_s$-dependent
        observables) --- all six are computed as
        $(g_s/{-}2)^n \times (\text{textbook leading term})$ with $n \in
        \{1, 2\}$. The triangulated $r_e/r_0 = 0.4994205099128317$ substitutes
        $g_s = -2.00231930\dots$ (the measured value, by which the joint fit
        defines $r_e$) and gets textbook agreement by construction.
  \item \textbf{2 at Bethe-estimate precision} (Lamb shift + helium ground
        state) --- the framework, where its non-relativistic-equivalent piece
        is exercised, reproduces the textbook Bethe-estimate calculation and
        inherits its residual against measurement.
\end{itemize}
