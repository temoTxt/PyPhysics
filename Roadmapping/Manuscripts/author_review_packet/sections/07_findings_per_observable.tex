\section{Six-observable cross-comparison + NIST-MCP provenance audit}
\label{sec:findings-per-observable}

The campaign's load-bearing quantitative comparison is the
\texttt{10\_CrossComparison.md}~\S2 six-observable table. The cross-check
column was added in PR~\#91 (issue~\#90) using the \texttt{nist} MCP
server~\cite{nist_mcp_pr91}; the column is a per-observable provenance
audit, not an independent corroboration.

\input{figures/cross_comparison_table}

\paragraph{Reading the table honestly.}
\begin{itemize}[topsep=2pt,itemsep=0pt]
  \item Only the electron $g_s$ row carries an authoritative independent
        source: CODATA-2018 via \texttt{scipy.constants}
        (\texttt{get\_constant}). The MCP-retrieved value matches the
        measurement column to 16 sig figs.
  \item The H $2P_{3/2}{-}2P_{1/2}$ row's MCP cross-check is the ASD
        \emph{theoretical}/Ritz level difference at $\approx 10{,}969.05$ MHz,
        \emph{not} an independent measurement. It differs from the measured
        $10{,}969.13(10)$~MHz by $\sim 0.08$~MHz. Do not read this as
        corroborating the framework.
  \item The H 1S hyperfine, He $^3P_0{-}^3P_1$, positronium ortho--para,
        and muonium hyperfine rows are served by the curated
        \texttt{list\_dirac\_targets} registry --- they restate the
        measurement column with provenance metadata, but are not independent
        of it.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Pattern is consistent --- and consistent by construction.}
All six observables share the same $(g_s/{-}2)^n$ scaling, and the
triangulated $r_e$ is by construction the joint-best-fit value across all
six. The ``six observables consistent'' pattern is therefore a single-fact
pattern (single-parameter joint fit) repeated six times, not six independent
corroborations.

\paragraph{Honest reading (verbatim from \texttt{10\_CrossComparison.md} \S2).}
\begin{quotation}
\noindent\textit{If the dual-theory framework is taken to require a
single-parameter $r_e$ calibrated against $g_s$, then the triangulated
$r_e \approx 0.4994205099\,r_0$ is the joint-best-fit value across the
$g_s$-dependent precision spectroscopy of hydrogen, helium, positronium,
and muonium. This is a self-consistency statement about parameter-fitting
within the framework, not an independent experimental discrimination of the
dual-theory framework from standard QED.}
\end{quotation}
