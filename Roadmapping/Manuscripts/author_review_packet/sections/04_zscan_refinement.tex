\section{Z-scan empirical refinement}
\label{sec:zscan-refinement}

\paragraph{Headline result (PR \#87, full derivation: \S\ref{sec:eq-zscan-fit}).}
The multi-$Z$ scan across $Z = 2, 6, 8, 14, 50$ supplies the
cross-$Z$ comparison the Li$^{2+}$ branches (\S\ref{sec:eq-li2plus-joint-fit}--\ref{sec:eq-li2plus-hyperfine}) could not run.
\[
\boxed{\;
  \chi^2_{\rm (Z\text{-}i)\;universal\;cutoff} \;=\; 1.0282\times 10^{16}
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{OUTCOME A DECISIVELY REJECTED.}
\;}
\]

\paragraph{Ion set + measurement provenance.}
$^3$He$^+$ (Schneider 2022 \emph{Nature} 606, 878);
$^{12}$C$^{5+}$ (Sturm 2014 \emph{Nature} 506, 467);
$^{16}$O$^{7+}$ (Verd\'u 2004 \emph{PRL} 92, 093002);

\paragraph{Per-$Z$ back-fit cutoff $x^{(Z)} = r_e^{(Z)}/r_0$.}
Inverting $g_r(x) = -2(1 + a)$ at each measured $g$:
\[
x^{(Z)} = \frac{2 - a_{\rm bound}^{(Z)}}{2(2 + a_{\rm bound}^{(Z)})}.
\]

\paragraph{Z-scaling fit (reading (Z-ii)).}
Fitting $x^{(Z)}$ vs.~$(Z\alpha)^2$:
\begin{align*}
\text{linear:}\quad     & x = 0.499\,383\,5903 + 0.176\,393\,13\,(Z\alpha)^2 \\
\text{quadratic:}\quad  & x = 0.499\,420\,6084 + 0.166\,275\,68\,(Z\alpha)^2
                          + 0.074\,154\,07\,(Z\alpha)^4
\end{align*}
\begin{itemize}[topsep=2pt,itemsep=0pt]
  \item Quadratic intercept $c_0 = 0.4994206084$ matches $x_{\rm univ} =
        0.4994205099$ to $9.8\times 10^{-8}$.
  \item Quadratic slope $c_2 = 0.166276$ matches QED's $1/6 = 0.166667$
        to $2.3\times 10^{-3}$.
\end{itemize}
The clean $(Z\alpha)^2$ form is a property of QED's bound-state
$g(Z\alpha) = 2[1 - (1/3)(Z\alpha)^2 - \dots]$, \emph{inherited} by the
back-fit --- not derived from the framework apparatus.

\paragraph{Outcome C (per PR \#87 conclusion, verbatim).}
\begin{itemize}[topsep=2pt,itemsep=0pt]
  \item Outcome~A (single universal cutoff) excluded at $\chi^2 \sim 10^{16}$
        over 5 ions.
  \item The (Z-ii) back-fit $x^{(Z)}$ is well-described by
        $x = c_0 + c_2(Z\alpha)^2 + c_4(Z\alpha)^4$ with $c_0 = x_{\rm univ}$
        recovered as $Z\to 0$ to $10^{-7}$, and $c_2 \approx 1/6$ ---
        the QED bound-state leading coefficient.
  \item The clean $(Z\alpha)^2$ form is QED-inherited, not framework-derived.
        Therefore \textbf{Outcome~C: $Z$-specific cutoff through QED-inheritance},
        extending PR~\#70's lepton-axis verdict (``particle-specific through
        $a_\ell$'') to the $Z$-axis (``$Z$-specific through
        $a_e^{\rm bound}(Z\alpha)$'').
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Reading.}
The Z-scan does not contradict the algebra of PRs~\#84/\#85/\#86 (the
(III.22) formula indeed has no explicit $Z$). What it shows is that the
framework's apparatus cannot \emph{derive} a Z-dependent inheritance curve ---
the framework supplies $g_r(x)$ and leaves each state's cutoff free
(per~(III.23)); the per-$Z$ back-fit lands on the QED bound-state curve
because that's the empirical content of $a_e^{\rm bound}(Z\alpha)$, supplied
to the inversion as input. The Branch~A vs.~Branch~C tension reduces to
which reading of (III.22)/(III.23) the senior author intends ---
\S\ref{sec:branch-a-vs-c}.
