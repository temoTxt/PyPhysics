\begin{abstract}
\noindent The Bethe--Salpeter precision-predictions campaign rederives 28
canonical results in atomic and few-electron physics under the proper-time /
dual-theory formulation of DRQM~I, comparing each prediction against
CODATA-2018 / PDG-2024 measured values. Of the 28 results, 20 are
formulation-independent (the framework agrees with textbook because it
reduces to textbook QM where the observable lives), six are
$g_s$-dependent observables back-fit to measured $g_s$ at the Z=1
triangulated $r_e/r_0$, and two are at Bethe-estimate precision (Lamb shift
and helium ground state). The campaign's load-bearing honest-scope statement,
preserved verbatim throughout: \textbf{zero of 28 results constitute
independent corroboration of the dual theory's content distinct from
textbook QM} (\texttt{10\_CrossComparison.md}~\S1).

\bigskip

\noindent The Z=1 triangulated cutoff value $r_e/r_0 = 0.4994205099128317
\pm 2.5\times 10^{-13}$ (PR~\#62) is the joint best-fit across the six
$g_s$-dependent observables under the only weighting that respects the
framework's Bethe-estimate precision floor. The closed-form algebraic
inversion of the (III.22) cutoff--anomaly identification yields
$r_e/r_0 = (2 - a_e)/(2(2 + a_e))$, which reproduces the triangulated value
to $3.45\times 10^{-13}$ when CODATA-full $a_e$ is supplied
(\texttt{Dual\_Relativistic\_Quantum\_Mechanics\_I.md}~\S III.D-extension).
Finding~2's verdict trajectory:
$\mathbin{\hbox{\color{red}\(\bullet\)}} \to

 \mathbin{\hbox{\color{yellow!90!black}\(\bullet\)}}/

at hypothesis-(ii) framework precision. Unconditional \(\checkmark\) requires
the proper-time photon-propagator re-derivation tracked in issue~\#75.

\bigskip

\noindent A multi-$Z$ scan across $Z = 2, 6, 8, 14, 50$ (PR~\#87, $^3$He$^+$
through $^{118}$Sn$^{49+}$) tests the (Z-i)~universal-cutoff vs.~(Z-ii)
$Z$-scaled-cutoff readings of (III.22)/(III.23). The (Z-i) hypothesis (single
$r_e/r_0$ fixed at the Z=1 triangulated value across all $Z$) is excluded at
$\chi^2 \approx 1.0\times 10^{16}$. The per-$Z$ back-fit
$x^{(Z)} = c_0 + c_2(Z\alpha)^2 + c_4(Z\alpha)^4$ recovers $c_0 = x_{\rm univ}$
(the Z=1 triangulated value) as $Z\to 0$ to $10^{-7}$, with
$c_2 \approx 1/6$ matching the QED bound-state leading coefficient. The
$(Z\alpha)^2$ form is a property of QED's $g(Z\alpha)$
\emph{inherited} by the back-fit, not derived from the framework apparatus.
The Z-axis verdict is Outcome~C (per-$Z$-specific via QED-inheritance),
analogous to PR~\#70's lepton-axis verdict (particle-specific via
$a_\ell$).

\bigskip

\noindent The Li$^{2+}$ Z-extension (PRs~\#84/\#85/\#86) reaches
``Branch~A by construction'' from the algebra (the cutoff formula has no $Z$),
but three of four Li$^{2+}$ measurements in the original brief were

The reconciliation is the focus of the closing question list
(\S\ref{sec:questions-for-tepper}).
