\section{AI-use disclosure (Crocco \S5)}
\label{sec:ai-use-disclosure}

Per
\href{https://doi.org/10.1177/15344843261445531}{Crocco, Rasdi \& Garavan
(2026), \emph{Responsible AI in Non-Empirical Research}}, and the repo's
implementation in
\texttt{Roadmapping/Tooling/CROCCO\_COMPLIANCE.md}, this packet was generated
with explicit AI involvement that is disclosed below in two categories.

\paragraph{Pragmatic AI uses (formatting, transcription, retrieval).}
\begin{itemize}[topsep=2pt,itemsep=0pt]
  \item Wolfram-MCP symbolic verification of each load-bearing equation in
        the source \texttt{.wl} notebooks. Inline \texttt{Wolfram-MCP}
        confirmation markers throughout the equations sections record the
        date of each cell's run. The \texttt{.wl} notebooks themselves are
        committed under
        \texttt{Roadmapping/Mathematica\_Notebooks/Quantum\_Mechanics/}.
  \item CODATA, NIST ASD, and PDG data retrieval via the \texttt{nist}~MCP
        server (issue~\#90, PR~\#91, \cite{nist_mcp_pr91}) for the
        cross-comparison column.
  \item Bibliography stub scaffolding via
        \texttt{Roadmapping/History/Bibliography/scaffold\_bib\_note.py}
        and \texttt{build\_bibtex.py}.
  \item Translation of source \texttt{.wl} notebook cells to LaTeX equation
        blocks (no derivation content changed; faithful transcription).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Substantive AI uses (synthesis, framing, prose drafting).}
\begin{itemize}[topsep=2pt,itemsep=0pt]
  \item Drafting of the packet's prose under explicit human direction.
        Prompt-of-record: \texttt{prompts/implementation\_prompt.md}.
  \item Selection of which load-bearing equations to surface in each
        \texttt{equations/} \texttt{.tex} block (the cells judged most
        relevant to the senior author's verdict question, not every cell of
        every notebook).
  \item Synthesis of the
        \hyperref[sec:derivation-chain]{derivation-chain section} (Dual Dirac
        Equation $\to$ each observable's prediction). The equations are
        transcribed verbatim from DRQM~I \S\S II--III and the BS section
        docs; the substantive moves are (a)~which subset of the chain to
        surface (the chain-rule step (III.6), the cutoff approximation
        (III.7), and the three (III.18)--(III.20) ``new terms'' are kept;
        spherical-coordinate algebra is summarised) and (b)~the per-observable
        $n_i$ summary table consolidating BS section docs in one place.
  \item The Branch~A vs.~Branch~C synthesis in
        \S\ref{sec:branch-a-vs-c} (``the algebra is correct as a statement
        about the formula; the empirical residual rejects Outcome~A;
        Outcome~C is the consistent residual''). This is a substantive
        interpretive move and is explicitly flagged.
  \item The outcome-matrix mapping in
        \S\ref{sec:open-theoretical-question} (A excluded by Z-scan,
        B excluded structurally, C the consistent residual, D unlikely
        without revising (III.22)).
  \item Selection of the six closing questions in
        \S\ref{sec:questions-for-tepper} from the larger pool of open
        questions inherited from the four BS PR bodies, the master \#67
        outcome-matrix issue, the FINDINGS~Finding~2 verdict trajectory,
        and the open theoretical issues (\#54, \#75).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Substantive AI not present in this packet.}
\begin{itemize}[topsep=2pt,itemsep=0pt]
  \item No new physics derivations are introduced. Every equation in the
        \texttt{equations/} sections is transcribed from a committed
        \texttt{.wl} notebook with its Wolfram-MCP confirmation preserved.
  \item No verdict on Branch~A vs.~Branch~C is announced by the packet;
        adjudication is deferred to the senior author per the closing
        question list.
  \item No abstract claim is made that the packet itself adjudicates the
        $r_e/r_0$ story. The packet's purpose is to surface the
        already-recorded findings in the form (PDF, LaTeX equations) the
        senior author actually reads.
\end{itemize}

human-authored \texttt{.wl} notebooks; the verdicts and the
honest-scope statements are human-authored and preserved verbatim. Any
substantive interpretive move (Branch~A vs.~Branch~C synthesis, outcome-matrix
mapping, closing-question selection) is flagged inline with

section for transparent review.
